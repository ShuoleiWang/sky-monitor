# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests and discussions, follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are treated
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove contributions or block participants who do not
follow these rules.
